Privacy and account data

Telegram sign-in and alerts

We use your Telegram ID to sign you in, link your accounts, and deliver alerts. To turn off private bot updates, send /stop to the TaliTrade bot.

Connected trading accounts

When you connect an account, TaliTrade imports account information and trade history to show your activity and challenge progress. Match-Trader connections use a read-only API token, stored encrypted on the server so TaliTrade can continue syncing. TaliTrade does not need permission to open, modify, or close trades.

Disconnect an account

Disconnect the account in TaliTrade to stop syncing. Revoke the token in Match-Trader or through your broker if you want to invalidate it with the provider. Turning off alerts does not disconnect your accounts.

Questions or removal requests

Email support@talitrade.com with questions about your data or to request removal. Keep your passwords and API tokens out of the email.
