A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you
jump in.

Who is TaliTrade for?+

TaliTrade is being built for prop firm traders who want their account information and trading history in one focused workspace.

Which platforms can I connect?+

The current focus is Match-Trader. Your broker must enable Trader API access for your account. Support for a platform does not mean every broker using it is supported.

Can TaliTrade place trades for me?+

This connection is for viewing and tracking data. Use a read-only API token. TaliTrade does not need permission to open, modify, or close your trades.

How much trade history will I see?+

Available history varies by broker and account. Your workspace is being designed to make imported coverage clear, so you know which period you're reviewing.

How do beta invites work?+

Early-access invitations will be sent in stages via Telegram. Joining the waitlist does not guarantee immediate access.
